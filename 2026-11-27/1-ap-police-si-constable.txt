TITLE
AP Police SI & Constable 2026 Apply Online | 378 SI, 827 Constable Posts

DESCRIPTION
AP Police SI and Constable 2026: online applications are open from 27 November 2026 (5 PM) to 16 December 2026 (5 PM). SLPRB Andhra Pradesh is recruiting 378 Sub-Inspectors (Civil 253, RSI AR 116, SPF 9), 827 Police Constables (Civil 542, AR 285), 158 Firemen and 171 Jail Warders.

SI: any degree, age 21 to 27 as on 1 July 2026, fee ₹600 (SC/ST ₹300).
Constable, Fireman, Warder: Intermediate, age 18 to 24 for constables and 18 to 32 for firemen and warders, fee ₹300 (SC/ST ₹150). SC, ST, BC and EWS get 5 years age relaxation.
Constable prelims: 31 January 2027. SI prelims: 28 February 2027.
